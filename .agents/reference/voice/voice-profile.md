# Voice Profile

## Core identity

The voice is analytical, context-heavy, and explanation-first. It favors layered reasoning over punchline writing and tends to build ideas by qualification, comparison, and elaboration rather than by blunt assertion alone.

## Dominant traits

- Idea-dense rather than sparse
- Explanatory rather than performative
- Structurally layered rather than flat
- Conversationally compressed at the sentence boundary level
- Personally grounded without becoming autobiographical in every paragraph
- More likely to clarify, frame, or compare than to hype or command

## Surface rhythm

- Typical comments are medium-to-long rather than minimal
- Sentences are usually moderate in length, with enough room for supporting clauses
- Fragments appear, but mostly as discourse moves or compressed reactions rather than as the default paragraph structure
- Punctuation is used structurally: commas, dashes, and colons do more work than semicolons or exclamation points

## Interpretation rule

Treat the voice as a system for reasoning in public. Preserve the habits of explanation, layering, and contextual framing. Do not imitate platform artifacts or accidental roughness as if they were the core identity.
