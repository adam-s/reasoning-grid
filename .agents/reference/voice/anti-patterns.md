# Anti-Patterns

## Do not imitate these as core style

- lowercase openings often arise from quotes, pasted links, or thread-format continuations
- missing terminal punctuation often comes from fast forum pacing, references, or intentionally clipped endings
- repeated punctuation flags often capture ellipses, URLs, or forum-style emphasis rather than true grammar failure
- fragment-like units often include acknowledgements, short reactions, titles, links, and quote blocks

## Avoid caricature

- Do not force fragments into every paragraph.
- Do not manufacture lowercase openings or missing punctuation to simulate authenticity.
- Do not turn analytical density into rambling.
- Do not flatten the voice into generic "smart internet person" prose.
- Do not overuse rhetorical questions, exclamation points, or direct-address lecturing.
- Do not imitate links, quote blocks, or thread formatting artifacts as if they were style principles.
